# FAQ: the six objections, answered honestly

**1. "Why switch to a new plugin from one person instead of a big vendor?"**
You shouldn't, unless your current setup is hurting you. What you get from a small vendor: the person who wrote the code answers your email within one business day (Mon–Fri), and every update can be rolled back in one click. What you give up: a smaller team and fewer features. If Bookly or Amelia works for you, stay.

**2. "Will I lose my appointments if I move?"**
That's the main thing we're built to prevent. The migration copies customers, services, staff and future appointments. Then you get a before/after count to check before you switch the booking form over. Your old plugin stays installed until you're satisfied.

**3. "What happens if I stop paying?"**
Your booking form keeps working. You stop getting updates and support. We never lock the dashboard or disable bookings when a license lapses.

**4. "Is the renewal price going to jump?"**
No. The renewal price is the price you paid, and it's printed on the pricing page. (We're telling you because both Amelia and LatePoint renew at the full list price after a first-year discount.)

**5. "Does it do everything Amelia/Bookly does?"**
No. We don't do events, gift cards or multisite yet. We focus on appointments for salons, barbers, tutors and therapists: staff schedules, multiple services in one appointment, payments, reminders and calendar sync. Check the feature list before you buy.

**6. "What if it doesn't work for me?"**
You get a full refund within 30 days, no argument. If we migrated you, we'll help you move back.

> Publish only the answers that are true in the shipped plugin. Each one is a promise.
