# SteadySlots vs Bookly (draft comparison page)

> **Before you publish:** every SteadySlots line in `[brackets]` is a promise. Publish it only once it's true in the shipped plugin. The Bookly facts are from Bookly's own pages (fetched 2026-10-18) and from public WordPress.org reviews. Re-check prices before publishing, because Bookly was running an "Autumn Sale".

## The short version
Bookly is a mature, widely used plugin (60,000+ active installs on WordPress.org, rated 88/100 from 585 ratings) with a polished booking form. If it works for you, stay. SteadySlots is for people who've been burned by a broken update or a slow support ticket and want to switch **without losing a single appointment**.

## Side by side

| | Bookly | SteadySlots |
|---|---|---|
| Entry paid price | Pro: $99/yr list, $39 on sale, or $129 lifetime list ($99 on sale), 1 site (Bookly pricing page) | [$79/yr, 1 site, unlimited staff] |
| Renewal price | "renews yearly". The renewal amount isn't stated in the fetched page text | [Same as you paid, printed on the pricing page] |
| Add-ons | Bookly says "No hidden costs or add-ons" and now sells bundled tiers (Pro, Business, Ultimate) | [One plan, every feature] |
| If your license lapses | Reviewers report dashboard nags and lockouts tied to the license (WP.org reviews `plugin-not-working-in-sweden-users`, `it-used-to-be-so-great`) | [Bookings keep working. You only lose updates and support] |
| Updates | Reviewers report updates breaking the plugin, e.g. "Whenever it's time to update, though, almost every time something breaks." (`updates-are-a-nightmare`, 2019) | [Automatic table backup and one-click rollback before every update] |
| Support | Reviewers report slow replies, e.g. "It's been 5 days since I started a support ticket…" (`it-is-helpful-5`, 2023) | [Named developer, first reply within 1 business day, Mon–Fri] |
| Moving your data | A reviewer: "still we aren't able to get the data out of the bookly system" (2020) | [Free done-for-you migration from Bookly: customers, services, staff, future appointments, with a before/after count] |
| Uninstall | A reviewer: "the Bookly plugin does not clear the database" (2024) | [Uninstall removes every table, documented] |
| Refunds | 30-day money-back guarantee (Bookly pricing page) | [30 days, no argument] |

## Fair notes
- Most add-on complaints about Bookly are from 2018 to 2023. Bookly's current pricing bundles add-ons into tiers.
- In the WordPress.org reviews we sampled, Bookly had no 1 to 3 star reviews in the last 12 months.
- Bookly has more features than SteadySlots: events, packages, chained services, multisite. If you need those, Bookly may be the better fit.
