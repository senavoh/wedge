# Outreach messages

**Rules:**
- **Never post these on WordPress.org.** Its guidelines say "The forums are not a venue to promote your own (or others') plugins, themes, websites or services." Contact people only through a channel they published themselves, such as their business or agency site's contact form.
- One message, and no follow-up spam.
- If they say no, thank them and stop.

## Message A: to a business owner or freelancer who left a public review about a broken update or slow support

> Subject: Your Amelia review about the [update / 409 error]
>
> Hi [name],
>
> I read your WordPress.org review about [their exact problem, in one line]. That sounds painful when clients are trying to book.
>
> I'm a WordPress developer building a small booking plugin for salons and solo practitioners, focused on two things: updates that can be rolled back in one click, and moving people off Amelia/Bookly without losing appointments.
>
> If it's useful, I'll migrate one site for free (customers, services, staff and future appointments, with a before/after count you can check). No obligation, and no review required.
>
> Either way, if you want, I'm happy to look at the [issue] and tell you what I think is causing it.
>
> [Your name]. I write the code and answer the email.
