Honest FAQ

Why trust a small plugin over a big vendor?

Only if your current setup hurts. You get the developer and a rollback button. You give up a big team and some features. If Amelia or Bookly works for you, stay.

Will I lose appointments when I move?

That’s the thing we’re built to prevent. You get a before/after count of customers, services and future appointments to check before you switch the form over.

Does it do everything Amelia or Bookly does?

No. No events, gift cards or multisite yet. It’s focused on appointments for small service businesses.

Is this available today?

Not yet. It’s in development. The waitlist tells us who to build the importers for first.

Where do the quotes come from?

Public WordPress.org reviews of Amelia, quoted word for word. We don’t name the reviewers here.
