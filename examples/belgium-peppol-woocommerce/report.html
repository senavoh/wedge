WooCommerce Peppol plugin for small Belgian web shops

Idea stress-test: a EUR 9-19/month WooCommerce plugin that sends and receives B2B invoices over Peppol, after Belgium's 1 January 2026 mandate.

01

Verdict

KILL

The mandate is real and in force (since 1 Jan 2026, tolerance ended 31 Mar 2026, Hermes shut 31 Dec 2025), but the job is already done for free or almost free: GoRoute's Woo plugin gives 50 invoices a month free with VAT/Peppol-ID lookup, WP Overnight's 300k-install plugin sends from about EUR 5/month, and accounting tools like Accountable receive for free. The pain left is setup friction (missing VAT numbers or Peppol IDs, UBL rejected by receivers), not something shops will pay EUR 9-19 a month for. Next best: sell fixed-price Peppol setup and fixes for Belgian Woo shops on top of the existing free connectors (untested).

The gap

The opening competitors leave, backed by what their customers say.

Belgian WooCommerce shops with occasional B2B buyers can't get Peppol invoices that go through first time from a Woo order, because the tools don't capture or look up the buyer's VAT number or Peppol ID and they produce UBL that receivers reject. GoRoute's free plugin already claims the lookup half.

The case against

Pricing and cost

Pricing

Don't charge EUR 9-19/mo for the plugin

Free tiers with certified Access Points sit under the proposed price, and they already cover the shop volumes of small B2B sellers.

Competitors: EUR 0 (GoRoute 50/mo, Peppyrus 100/mo, Accountable) to EUR 15/mo (B2Brouter); WP Overnight about EUR 5-10/mo
