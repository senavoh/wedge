# Gaps (quick)
Gap sentence: Belgian WooCommerce shops with occasional B2B buyers can't get Peppol invoices that go through first time from a Woo order, because the tools don't capture or look up the buyer's VAT number / Peppol ID at checkout and produce UBL that receivers reject (e31-e38).

| Opportunity | pain | wtp | gap | fit | reach | build | total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Woo Peppol send+receive plugin, €9-19/mo | 5 | 4 | 2 | 8 | 4 | 6 | 46 |

- pain 5: repeated friction from different authors on 3 sources (e31-e40, e23-e28), nobody leaving.
- wtp 4: people pay for substitutes (WPO credits e14, B2Brouter €15 e16), but the loud complaint is about having to pay at all (e25, e26); free tiers cap the price (e15, e21).
- competition_gap 2: free/cheap Woo plugins with certified APs already exist (e15, e18, e19), the 300k-install incumbent ships Peppol (e12-e14), receiving is free elsewhere (e20, e21), Hermes is gone but not needed (e7).
- founder_fit 8: PHP/Woo dev, fits budget; needs an AP partner.
- reachability 4: WP.org forums ban self-promotion; no named free Belgian Woo merchant community found; reachable mainly through accountants or paid search.
- build_cost 6: a few weeks on an AP API, plus ongoing validation upkeep.
Verdict pre-critic: KILL (pain < 6).
