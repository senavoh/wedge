# Pains (quick)
Customer-voice sources: WP.org support threads (WPO and others), Shopify reviews (Sufio, POP), HN, Belgian press (NSZ survey). Reddit unavailable. ~300 raw records; most WP.org Peppol plugins have 0 reviews.

1. **Peppol costs money for tiny firms** (medium): HN e23-e26, NSZ press e27-e28. Counter: e29, e30 (cheap/free options exist). This is about the mandate, not about Woo tooling; free tools answer it.
2. **Buyer VAT number / Peppol ID not captured from the order** (medium): Sufio e31-e32, WP.org e33-e35. Leads to failed sends found after the fact.
3. **Generated UBL rejected by receiving software** (medium): WP.org e36-e40 (Belgian 6% VAT, Moneybird, Accountable, regenerated UBL).
4. **Demand signals** (low): merchants asking "Peppol ready?" e41-e43; competitors praised e44, e45.
No one in the evidence threatens to leave a tool over this; complaints are friction and bug reports.
Most likely to switch: #2 and #3 (failed sends), but GoRoute/Peppol Plugged already market fixes for both.
