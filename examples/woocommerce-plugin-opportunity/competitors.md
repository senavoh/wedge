- Official plugins: broad reach, sync + ads + pixel bundled; weak at large catalogs and reliability (pain-sync-overload, pain-sync-fails, pain-ads-coupling).
- Paid feed plugins: well rated, recommended by unhappy users of rivals (e24, e25, e26, e27); weak at stuck feeds on big catalogs (e4, e5, e6, e9) and license/refund trust (e19, e20, e23).
- Crowded: Google Shopping XML feeds for small/medium stores (free tiers do it).
- Thinner: throttled, batched, observable sync for 10k-50k SKU stores; "feed only" without ads coupling (e16, e17). Hypothesis, not proven empty.
