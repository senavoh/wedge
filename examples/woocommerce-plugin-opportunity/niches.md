# Niches (quick, 3)
Sources: HN (8 seeds, 190 records, thin for WooCommerce), WordPress.org plugin pages + API stats, 1-2 star review threads (531 records), woocommerce.com and vendor pages. Reddit blocked.

1. **Large-catalog stores syncing products to Meta/Google** — buyer: WooCommerce stores with 1k+ SKUs selling via Facebook/Instagram Shops and Google Merchant Center. Signal: Meta for WooCommerce 400k installs, 2.1 stars, 315 of 481 ratings are 1-star; Google for WooCommerce 800k installs, 2.7 stars, 132 of 268 1-star (WP.org API, 2026-10-18). Stuck/overloaded sync recurs across Meta, AdTribes and CTX reviews (e1, e2, e4, e5, e6). Served by: Meta for WooCommerce, Google for WooCommerce (free, official), Product Feed PRO (AdTribes), CTX Feed. Fit: PHP, Action Scheduler, WC data stores — core Woo skills.
2. **Product add-ons / custom options** — buyer: stores selling configurable products. Signal: PPOM price jump (e28), broken add-on pricing (e30), removed features (e31); official Product Add-Ons 3.4/5 (49 reviews) at $79/yr; wc-kalkulator closed 2026-06-21 (security). But most PPOM complaints are 5+ years old. Served by: ThemeHigh Extra Product Options (4.9, 219 ratings), Advanced Product Fields (4.8, 284), PPOM, YITH add-ons (3.7), WooCommerce Product Add-Ons. Fit: excellent (pure PHP).
3. **Booster for WooCommerce lifetime-license holders** (repriced) — lifetime support ended 2020, support now $129.99/yr (e32, e33, e34). Booster 30k installs, 4.6 stars. Served by: dozens of single-purpose plugins per module. Fit: good, but a 100-module replacement is not a 15h/week build.

Dropped: wishlists (TI 4.7/500 ratings, WPC 4.8 cover YITH's 3.9), product filters (WBW 4.8/325, Filter Everything 4.6, HUSKY 4.6 cover YITH's 3.5), booking/events (excluded by founder). No large paid WooCommerce plugin was found closed in 2025-26; closures found were small plugins.
