# FAQ (honest answers)

**Why pay you when Stockroom is free?** You might not need to. If your vendors are already suppliers and your printer works, install it yourself. You pay us for the hours: mapping vendors, importing history, testing labels, training staff.

**Is this an app?** No. It's a one-time setup on tools you choose. We don't lock you into anything.

**What if my Stocky data is gone?** Shopify's read-only window ends Nov 29, 2026 (per a vendor's migration checklist [k1]). After that, we rebuild from your Shopify product export and supplier invoices.

**Will you touch my live inventory?** Only after you sign off. We test on one location first.

**How long does it take?** The target is under a week for one location. We'll tell you up front if yours is bigger.

**What if it doesn't work?** If your first PO doesn't go through end to end, you get your money back.
