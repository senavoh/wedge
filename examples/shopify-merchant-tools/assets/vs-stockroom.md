# Shelf Sorted vs Stockroom (draft comparison page)

**Short version:** Stockroom is a free app. Shelf Sorted is a one-time setup service, and we often set you up *on* Stockroom. So this isn't either/or.

| | Stockroom (MyWorks) | Shelf Sorted |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Shopify app for POs, receiving, counts, labels | A person who sets up your purchasing and receiving with you |
| Price | Free, per its listing [c1, c3] | $199 once (draft) |
| Stocky migration | "Migrate from Stocky." per its listing [c2] | We do the migration with you, including vendors that never made it into Shopify as suppliers |
| Label printing | Listed [c1] | We test your actual printer and label stock before we finish |
| Staff training | Self-serve | A recorded walkthrough for your team |
| Ongoing cost | $0 | $0 |

**When you don't need us:** if your catalog is small, your vendors are already set up as suppliers, and your label printer works, install Stockroom and you're done.

**When we help:** you have dozens of vendors, years of POs, and a team that needs to receive boxes tomorrow.

*Every Stockroom claim above comes from its own App Store listing, fetched 2026-10-17. Re-check it before publishing.*
