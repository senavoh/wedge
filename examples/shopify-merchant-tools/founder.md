## How this filters the search

- **Buyer:** other Shopify merchants. The founder's unfair advantage is being one: he knows the admin, the apps and the daily chores.
- **Build ceiling:** a simple embedded app or a service. No billing engines, no checkout rewrites, nothing where one bug costs a merchant real money every day (that rules out payments-heavy categories as a first project).
- **Time:** 10 hours a week means the first paid version must ship in weeks, not months.
- **Risk:** side income first, so a cheap test with a clear pass/fail beats a long build.
