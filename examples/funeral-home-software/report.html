Verdict

KILL

The market is crowded and cheap. About 30 products are listed, with tiers from $49 to $79 a month, a free version and month-to-month terms. Most of the director reviews this run could read praise their current tool. The one sharp complaint, a freezing mobile app, came from a single reviewer, and Gather already sells phone case access. Capterra, G2 and Reddit returned 403, so price and switching complaints there went unread. A cheap check is 10 calls with directors before writing any code.

07

7-day test

One experiment, one pass bar. Run it before writing code.

Before writing code, call 10 owners of independent homes (1 to 3 directors, found through state funeral director association member lists). Ask what made them switch software last time, what they pay, and whether mobile case access fails them.

Pass bar (7 days): 6 of 10 name the same unsolved problem and say they would switch for it at $79 to $149 a month

  • Day 1-2: pull 30 independent homes from one state association directory
  • Day 3-6: call or visit, aiming for 10 conversations
  • Day 7: tally; kill if there is no shared problem

Kill it if

  • Fewer than 6 of 10 directors share one unsolved problem
  • Most are happy with a sub-$100/mo tool
