# Gaps (quick)

Candidate gap: Indie founders who lost GummySearch can't get trustworthy customer-pain research because every option either depends on gated Reddit access (e12, e16-e19) or scores ideas with flattering, unsourced AI output (e21-e28).

Problem: the "doesn't depend on Reddit" half is already sold at the same price to the same buyer (GripeFind $29/mo, "Coming from GummySearch?", c1-c3), and the "verified sources" half is sold by DimeADozen at $9 one-time (c8-c10). Free substitutes: ChatGPT/Claude, ValidatorAI (c11), F5Bot (c6), the open-source Wedge skill itself, and DIY scripts (e34, e35).
