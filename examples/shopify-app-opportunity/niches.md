# Niches (quick, 3)

Method: 8 HN seeds (raw/scout.jsonl, 199 HN records, mostly noise) + ~15 Shopify Community searches via Discourse search.json (174 topics) + a scan of 155 App Store category pages and the newest 1-2 star review pages of 55 large apps. Structural finding: nearly every paid category leader is rated 4.7-5.0. The badly rated "top apps" are mostly Shopify's own free apps (Retail Barcode Labels 2.3, Shopify Order Printer 3.6, Search & Discovery 2.6, Shopify Subscriptions 3.7, Shopify Bundles), each already flanked by well-rated paid apps.

1. **Stocky refugees (POS Pro retailers: POs, receiving, stocktakes, labels).** Trigger: Shopify shut Stocky on Aug 31 2026. Signal: dozens of Community threads (Feb-Oct 2026), Stocky rated 3.0 (211). e1-e7, e9. Serving now: Shopify native POs + POS Quick count (free), Prediko, Stockie, Sumtracker, Stockroom, PML Stock Take, EasyScan, plus ~10 new 2026 entrants (Binly, Alfred, EZstock, Replenly, Inmana, Stockyard, Larderia...). Fit: inventory CRUD on Admin GraphQL + Polaris, exactly the founder's stack.
2. **Barcode label printing for retail (leaving Shopify's Retail Barcode Labels).** Signal: RBL 2.3 stars, 293 of 476 reviews are 1-2 stars, ~20 low reviews in 2026; Community threads. e13-e19. Serving now: ReFo (5.0, 572), Yanet (4.9, 456), BarcodeMan, EasyScan, LabelCraft, Label Wizard, F: Retail Barcode Generator. Fit: small app, weeks of work.
3. **Subscriptions for small merchants (leaving Bold v2 / Shopify Subscriptions).** Signal: Bold migration double-charges, Shopify Subscriptions 3.7 (27% 1-2 star). e25-e28. Serving now: Appstle (5.0, 9,042), Seal (4.9, 3,146), Loop (5.0, 788). Fit: weak; payments edge cases and support load for 20 h/week.

Dropped: product options (Globo 4.9/5,172, OPTIS 4.9), PDF invoices (Order Printer Pro 4.9/2,989, Sufio 4.9), accounting sync (A2X 4.9, Webgility 4.9), loyalty (Smile 4.9/4,642): loved incumbents. Generic "app price hike" anger (300% increase thread) names no category. Reddit unavailable (blocked).
