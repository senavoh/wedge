# Critic: receive-to-label for Stocky refugees on native POs

Searched 2026-10-18. Sources saved in raw/critic.jsonl (Shopify Community threads, shopify.dev changelog, Community guidelines, HN). help.shopify.com returned HTTP 403 (both the transitioning-from-stocky page and the purchase-orders page). apps.shopify.com search returned only a login wall, so App Store listings and review counts were not checked here.

Bottom line: the problem is real, but so is the competition. The one structural moat the idea had (apps "can't read native POs") went away on Sept 17 2026 when Shopify shipped a public Purchase Order Read API. Since then any of the 8+ apps already pitching in these threads can add the exact feature. Free offerings have already set the price anchor at $0.

- **1. Someone tried and died. Severity: low.**
  Found: Genie Inventory shut down on Aug 31 2025 by merging with Doss, not by failing in this niche: "Just found out Genie Inventory is officially shutting down on August 31, 2025 (they’re merging with Doss)." https://community.shopify.com/t/genie-inventory-is-sunsetting/551746. Stocky itself is the main corpse, and Shopify killed it on purpose. The searches "app shutting down barcode" and "barcode label app discontinued" turned up no third-party receive-to-label app that died. HN has general warnings about Shopify delisting: "Existing developers with successful apps are suddenly delisted from the app store or even shut down entirely." https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=32042530.
  Rebuttal: this niche has no graveyard. Its problem is too many entrants (see 5), not dead ones. Resolved as weak.

- **2. Free incumbent (Shopify native). Severity: medium.**
  Found: as of the threads' latest replies, Shopify has announced no native PO label printing. The 622687 summary says "No native Shopify fix was announced" (https://community.shopify.com/t/feature-request-print-barcode-labels-directly-from-purchase-orders-like-stocky/622687), and 669060 says "No native Shopify solution has been confirmed." (https://community.shopify.com/t/allow-barcode-label-printing-directly-from-a-purchase-order-or-transfer-number/669060). Shopify is clearly investing in physical inventory, though. The changelog's "Physical inventory feature preview" (July 17 2026) covers "bins, counts, and purchase orders" (https://shopify.dev/changelog/posts/physical-inventory-feature-preview). Shopify also owns Retail Barcode Labels, and merchants are asking for exactly this feature in it: "Add the ability to enter a PO number or Transfer number in the Retail Barcode Labels app" (669060). Adding a "print received items" button to its own label app would be cheap for Shopify. One app dev argued the opposite: "they probably won’t as that would require a lot of functionality they do not currently offer" (622687). The help page could not be checked (403).
  Unresolved. No announcement yet, but Shopify owns both halves (POs and RBL), and its bins/counts work shows the inventory team is shipping.

- **3. Pain is real but nobody pays. Severity: high.**
  Found: the price anchor is $0. MyWorks/Stockroom: "we do support label printing in the PO app we recently built - completely free, to help merchants as Stocky goes away." https://community.shopify.com/t/csv-download-from-the-new-pos-creation-replacing-stocky-it-was-a-quick-and-easy-way-to-create-barcoded-labels-for-all-received-stock-items/632194. LabelCraft and FlochLabels both offer "Free up to 200 labels a month" (632194, 639374). Merchants resent paying at all: "object to needing paid third-party apps to restore functionality that was previously included" (summary, https://community.shopify.com/t/shopify-kills-inventory-management/639203). New entrants are giving months away to get testers: Restock Ops offers "3 months free instead of only the standard 14-day trial" (summary, https://community.shopify.com/t/left-stocky-looking-for-10-small-pos-retailers-to-test-restock-ops-pos-partial-receiving-labels-scanner-stocktakes/695918). This adds to the pre-existing e20/e21 ceilings ($40 "insulting", nothing over $100).
  Unresolved. The pain is real, but a label-only wedge competes against free label printing that is already built into free PO apps.

- **4. Channel closed. Severity: high.**
  Found: the Community guidelines ban app promotion outside one board: "App recommendations are allowed only on the Ask and Offer board." and prohibit "Recommending or promoting apps outside the Ask and Offer board, even when an app is relevant to the question or the member explicitly asks for recommendations." https://community.shopify.com/guidelines. That removes the plan's main reach channel (replying in the 10+ Stocky threads), though older threads are full of vendor replies. A new PO app's founder describes the 0-review problem: "Launched my first Shopify app a week ago (Purser), still on 0 reviews." The same thread notes that "Shopify policy prohibits incentivizing reviews" (summary). https://community.shopify.com/t/launched-my-first-shopify-app-a-week-ago-purser-still-on-0-reviews-how-would-you-approach-the-first-installs/654059. The urgency window is also over: Stocky shut Aug 31, and most refugees have already picked something. One thread is titled "Stocky is gone - three things that catch people out afterwards, whichever replacement you picked" (topic 676430, Sept 4). App Store ranking could not be checked (login wall).
  Partial rebuttal: Ask and Offer is still allowed, and Restock Ops was recruiting there on Oct 10. Unresolved overall.

- **5. Copyable in a week. Severity: high.**
  Found: it has already been copied, many times. FyreTrail: "FyreTrail allows you to print ONLY what has been delivered for an order." https://community.shopify.com/t/trying-to-weed-through-the-suggestions-for-a-stocky-replacement/639374. LabelCraft "reads a received Transfer or Shipment directly and prints exact-size labels for every line item" (632194). FlochLabels prints from "transfers, purchase orders, and CSVs" (https://community.shopify.com/t/print-barcode-labels-from-purchase-orders-anyone-else/638568). Restock Ops has "barcode labels from the receipt" (695918). Also named in these threads: Stockroom (free), labelloom, Binly, Replenly, ShelfSprint. A dev in 638568 tested the received-quantity data path end to end: "the API returned acceptedQuantity=6 with origin.name set to the supplier from the PO." Once the PO Read API shipped (see 6), reading native POs takes a scope request and a query.
  Unresolved. Nothing here is defensible. The only possible edges are UX quality, printer support, and price.

- **6. Platform risk (PO API). Severity: low now (it was high).**
  Found: through summer 2026 the Community consensus was "there’s still no Purchase Order API, so apps simply can’t see PO data at all." (https://community.shopify.com/t/api-endpoint-coming-soon-for-purchase-orders-with-stocky-sunset/587136) and "there’s no PurchaseOrder object in the Admin API" (638568). That changed on Sept 17 2026: "You can now read purchase orders through the inventory Purchase Order and inventory Purchase Orders queries in GraphQL Admin API version 2026-10 and later." The release is limited, though: "This is a read-only release. Purchase order mutations are not included." https://shopify.dev/changelog/posts/purchase-order-read-api. Received quantities come from linked InventoryShipments, and the changelog also lists "Adding a canceled receive action to the inventory shipments API" (Sept 17) (https://shopify.dev/changelog).
  Rebuttal: a read-only API plus the shipments API is enough for receive-to-label, so the build is feasible. The bad news is that this rebuttal also wipes out the only barrier described in risk 5. Remaining risk: the API is brand new (2026-10 version), and an app that cannot write POs leaves receiving in Shopify's UI.

## Net
Severity count: 3 high (WTP, channel, copyability), 1 medium (native), 2 low. The original "gap 3" score was generous, since the gap is now about 0 for a label-only product. The verdict stays PIVOT. A viable angle would need something the free apps don't do (for example a POS-tile receive-and-print flow for a specific printer fleet, or a vertical such as apparel size runs), not "Stocky's label button again".
