- Pricing: $300-$500 fixed setup, plus an optional $15/mo maintenance. Anchors: merchants were pushed toward ERPs at $350-$1,000+/mo (e46, unverified single source); public apps run $0-$19/mo (e35, e41).

- 7-day test: post one Ask and Offer listing and answer 10 Stocky/label threads helpfully. Pass: 3 merchants book a call and 1 pays a deposit. Kill: 0 deposits after 14 days, or every lead says a free app already covers it.
