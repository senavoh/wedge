01

Verdict

KILL

Expired certificates do take sites down, and Let's Encrypt ending its expiry emails in 2025 left some people without a warning. But the exact product, an expiry email, is already free from Red Sift (250 certificates, recommended by Let's Encrypt), HetrixTools, StatusCake, LetsMonitor and self-hosted Uptime Kuma, and the people who hit this pain are developers who solve it with a script. A prior SSL monitor, SSLPing, shut down after its creator never dared to charge. Don't build this as a standalone $9/month product.

Answer: Unresolved.

Answer: Unresolved.

Risk: Copyable in a week: every uptime tool already ships it as a checkbox.

Answer: Unresolved.

Risk: The buyer you named mostly doesn't have the problem: hosted site builders such as Squarespace give automatic SSL.

Answer: Unresolved.

Risk: Shorter certificate lifetimes (47 days) could raise urgency.

Answer: The industry's answer is more automation (Let's Encrypt says so, o8), plus monitoring that is already free. More renewals mean more silent failures, but that still doesn't open a paid gap (e28, e31).

Pricing and cost

Pricing

$9/month is not supportable for the alert alone

The core alert costs $0 elsewhere, and the evidence shows people leaving paid monitoring for free tools, not paying more.

Competitors: $0 (Red Sift, HetrixTools, LetsMonitor, StatusCake) to $15/month (Cert Spotter)

09

7-day test

One experiment, one pass bar. Run it before writing code.

Only if you still want to try: a one-page pre-sale aimed at small web agencies (multi-client SSL, domain and uptime report, white-label), plus 20 direct conversations.

Pass bar (7 days): 5 paid pre-orders at $9 or more per month

  • Day 1: list 40 small web agencies that publish their client portfolios
  • Days 2-5: 20 conversations about how they track client certificates and domains today
  • Days 6-7: ask for pre-payment; count the yes answers

Kill it if

  • Fewer than 5 paid pre-orders after 7 days
  • Most agencies say they already use HetrixTools, UptimeRobot or Uptime Kuma
