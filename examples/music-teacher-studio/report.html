01

Verdict

KILL

The admin pain is real, but it's covered. Teachers complain about makeups, reschedules and late tuition, yet My Music Staff already does makeup credits, self-rescheduling and auto-invoicing for $16.95/mo, Nova is free up to 10 students, and many teachers say Venmo and Excel are enough. The best open gap, practice logs that teachers can correct, comes from three reviews of a single app, in a niche whose best-known app (Tonara) shut down in 2023. Caveat: Capterra, G2, Trustpilot and Reddit were blocked from this network, so complaints about incumbents on those sites weren't read.

Pricing and cost

Pricing

If tested: free for up to 10 students, then $3-5/mo

Solo-studio tools are already $0-17/mo, and teachers are cutting subscriptions. A practice add-on only works priced well below the studio tool it sits next to.

Competitors: $0 (Nova free) to $16.95/mo (MMS); $99+ for schools (Opus1)

09

7-day test

One experiment, one pass bar. Run it before writing code.

Before writing code, ask 15 teachers in your own groups who use Practice Space or a studio tool's practice log whether broken logs cost them time, and whether they'd pay $3-5/mo to fix it.

Pass bar (7 days): 8 of 15 say it happens weekly and 3 ask to try it

  • Day 1: write a 3-question ask with no link
  • Days 2-4: send it to 15 teachers you know from your groups
  • Day 5: count weekly-pain answers and asks to try
  • Day 7: go or stop

Kill it if

  • Fewer than 5 of 15 report broken practice logs as a weekly problem
  • Nobody asks to try it
  • Teachers say the My Music Staff or Nova practice log already does this
