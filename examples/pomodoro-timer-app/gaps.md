# Gaps
Gap: Students who want a simple, good-looking focus timer can't get one that stays out of the way, because the big apps now push subscriptions, ads or clutter on top of the timer. (e1,e2,e9,e10,e14,e15,e17)
| Opportunity | pain | wtp | gap | fit | reach | build | total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $4.99/mo student timer with design, streaks, stats | 6 | 4 | 3 | 8 | 5 | 9 | 54 |
- pain 6: many authors across 6 apps, some cancel/leave (e5,e12,e18), but App Store is the only source type.
- wtp 4: people pay small one-time sums (e13,e19,e20) and reject subscriptions (e2,e7). $4.99/mo runs straight into that.
- gap 3: streaks/stats/design already free in Forest, Flora, FocusPomo, FLIP (e23,e25-e27).
- fit 8, build 9: SwiftUI solo dev, weeks of work, under $500.
- reach 5: App Store search is crowded; student communities (Reddit study subs, TikTok) not verified here.
Verdict: PIVOT (pain >= 6, GO fails on wtp and total). Pivot: price + angle.
