Verdict

PIVOT

The pain is real: students are angry right now at focus apps that added subscriptions, ads and clutter, Forest above all. But the pitch sells features (streaks, stats, design) that Forest, Flora, FocusPomo and FLIP already give away, and a $4.99/month subscription is the exact thing reviewers are rejecting. Pivot the price and the angle: a one-tap, ad-free timer sold once, aimed at people leaving Forest.

How to win

Specific moves that pull customers to you instead of them.

A focus timer that starts in one tap. No ads, no subscription.

Switching triggers

Forest's subscription and UI update (Sep-Oct 2026)

→ Be the answer when people ask for a Forest alternative; show one-tap start and pay-once pricing up front.

  • “cancelled my subscription planting has become less exciting and more tedious.”
    appstore · 2026-09-28 verified

Offers that win switchers

  • One-time unlock, no subscription
  • No ads ever, free tier included
  • Promise that no feature moves behind a paywall later

Build first

  1. One-tap start and repeat, including from the lock screen Answers pain: bloat.
  2. Ad-free free tier Answers pain: ads.
  3. Local streaks and stats Table stakes, not a differentiator.

Where unhappy customers are

  • Forest App Store reviews Fresh subscription and UI complaints
  • Study Bunny App Store reviews Students complaining about ads

Trust signals to show

  • Price shown on the App Store page
  • Written no-paywall-later promise

Do not copy

  • Ads in a study app
  • Paywalling features users already had
  • Several screens before a session starts

Pricing and cost

Pricing

Free, ad-free tier plus a one-time unlock around $4.99 to $9.99

Reviewers pay small one-time sums and reject subscriptions (e2, e13, e19). A monthly price runs into the category's loudest complaint.

Competitors: Free to $19.99 per 3 months; users mention paying $10 lifetime

09

7-day test

One experiment, one pass bar. Run it before writing code.

TestFlight beta of a one-tap, ad-free timer, offered to people looking for a Forest alternative.

Pass bar (7 days): 100 beta signups and 20 people who say they would pay once

  • Day 1-3: build one-tap timer with local streaks in SwiftUI
  • Day 4: TestFlight link and a one-page site
  • Day 5-7: share it where students ask for Forest alternatives, following each community's rules

Kill it if

  • Under 30 beta signups after 7 days
  • Testers say they would only use it free
