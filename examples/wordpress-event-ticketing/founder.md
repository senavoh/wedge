# Founder fit

- **Budget:** under $1k
- **Skills:** builds web apps (with AI help)
- **Hours:** 10 per week
- **Risk:** side income first
- **Channel:** online only

Filters applied downstream: software only, no hardware, no regulated/licensed work, no local sales, no product needing a sales team or months of build. Prefer buyers who already pay for a tool and gather in public, free places (app marketplaces, forums, HN).
