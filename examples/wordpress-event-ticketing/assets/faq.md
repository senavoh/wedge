# FAQ: the top objections, answered honestly

**1. Ticket Spot and Ticket Tailor already do hosted checkout on WordPress. Why you?**
They do: Ticket Spot says a theme update "cannot corrupt an order or interrupt a sale" (k1), and Ticket Tailor offers an embedded WordPress checkout (k5). We're built for agencies managing many client sites, with a single dashboard and one flat price. If you run one organization's site, they may suit you better.

**2. Zeffy is free for nonprofits. Why pay?**
If your client is a registered nonprofit and is happy on Zeffy's hosted pages, they probably shouldn't pay us (k7).

**3. What if you go out of business like Brown Paper Tickets or Tickets.ie?**
Fair worry (k9, k10). We never hold ticket money: it goes straight into your client's own Stripe account. Attendee data can be exported as CSV at any time.

**4. Who handles chargebacks and refunds?**
Your client does, through their own Stripe account, because we use direct charges. Refunds for cancelled events are issued from their Stripe balance.

**5. Will a WordPress update break it?**
Checkout and ticket emails run on our servers, not in WordPress. Our own outages can still happen; they'll show on a public status page.

**6. Do you do calendars, seating and recurring events?**
Not at launch. Keep your calendar plugin if you need one; we only sell and deliver tickets.
