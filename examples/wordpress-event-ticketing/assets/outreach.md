# Outreach (agencies and freelancers only, through their own public channels)

Rules: never post or DM in WordPress.org forums. Its guidelines say the forums "are not a venue to promote your own (or others’) plugins, themes, websites or services" (k8). Contact people only through the contact page on their own website. Send one message, with no follow-up spam.

## Message 1: an agency that publicly wrote about an events plugin breaking client sites
Subject: events plugin breaking client sites

Hi [name], I read your review of [plugin] on WordPress.org about updates breaking your clients' sites. I'm building a small ticketing tool for agencies where checkout runs off-site, so a WordPress update can't take a client's ticket sales down, and you see every ticket sent across all clients in one place. It isn't live yet. Would you be up for a 15-minute call so I can learn what that problem costs you? There's no pitch, and I'll send you my notes either way.
[Your name], [site]
