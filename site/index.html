Evidence,
or it didn't happen.

AI is great at sounding sure. Wedge re-opens every page it cites and checks the quote is really there. Made-up quotes get caught before they reach your report.

  • Two sources per claim

    A claim needs two checked sources from different pages, or it's labeled unverified.

  • A critic that argues back

    A second pass tries to kill the top idea before it's ranked: who tried and failed, free rivals, people who complain but won't pay.

  • No API keys

    Free sources only: Reddit, Hacker News, App Store reviews, GitHub issues, pricing pages and web search.

Start
wherever you are.

Four ways in. Quick is the default, a few minutes. Ask for deep when you want the full run.

  • From scratch

    “Find me a business I can start.”

    Five quick questions about your budget, skills and time, then niches with real demand that fit you.

  • From what you know

    “I’m a dental hygienist. What could I build?”

    Starts from your industry and the access you already have.

  • Stress-test an idea

    “Is a dog-walking app for apartment buildings any good?”

    Your idea against real complaints and real competitors. It will tell you to walk away if it should.

  • Beat my competitors

    “How do I take customers from Jobber?”

    For a business you already run: when customers leave them, and how to be there.

How a run
works.

Ten stages in four steps. Progress is saved as it goes, so you can stop and pick it up later, even in a different agent.

  1. 1

    Research

    Asks about you, finds niches, maps competitors with real prices, and reads hundreds of reviews and complaint threads.

  2. 2

    Decide

    Scores each opportunity on six factors with the reasoning shown, then a critic tries to kill the winner. Verdict: GO, PIVOT or KILL, or INCONCLUSIVE if it couldn't reach real customers.

  3. 3

    Win

    Turns competitor weaknesses into a plan: when customers switch, what to offer them, where to find them, and what to build first.

  4. 4

    Prove

    Checks every quote, then writes one shareable report, a launch kit with a 7-day test, and a share image.

GO PIVOT KILL INCONCLUSIVE

Honest verdicts. It will tell you to pivot or walk away, and show why. No customer evidence means no guess.

    How to win.

    A gap only matters if you can get customers through it. The win plan turns competitor weaknesses into moves you can make this week.

    • Switching moments

      When people leave: a price hike, a new limit, a bad update. Each one backed by quotes.

    • Positioning in their words

      Lifted from the complaints themselves, not a brainstorm.

    • Offers that pull switchers

      Free import, locked prices, no contracts: whatever removes their reason to stay.

    • Where they hang out

      The specific communities and review pages where unhappy customers already talk.

    • Ready-to-edit drafts

      A comparison page, outreach messages, ad copy and an FAQ.

    • A monthly check-in

      Re-checks competitors for new complaints, price changes and new rivals.

    Install in
    one command.

    No accounts, no API keys. Needs Python 3, which Macs and most Linux machines already have.

    Installs every skill into the agents on your machine. Needs Node.js 22 or newer; on older versions use the Script tab.

    $ npx skills add senavoh/wedge

    Inside Claude Code, add the marketplace, then install the plugin.

    /plugin marketplace add senavoh/wedge
    /plugin install wedge@wedge

    Copies the skills into every agent folder it finds. Read the script first.

    $ curl -fsSL https://getwedge.dev/install.sh | sh

    Clone the repo and copy the skills into your agent's skills folder.

    $ git clone https://github.com/senavoh/wedge
    $ cp -r wedge/skills/* ~/.claude/skills/

    That path is for Claude Code. Use your own agent's skills folder.

    Then ask your agent

    “Use wedge to find me a business I can start with $500 and 10 hours a week.”

    Questions
    and answers.

    Is it free?

    Yes. Wedge is open source under the MIT license, needs no API keys and uses only free data sources. The only cost is your agent's own usage.

    Which agents does it work with?

    Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI, OpenCode, and any agent that reads SKILL.md files.

    Can it make things up?

    It can try, but it won't get far. Every quote is re-checked against its source. Anything that can't be confirmed is labeled, and unconfirmed claims can't drive the verdict.

    Does it scrape sites like G2 or Capterra?

    No. Those only come in through ordinary web search results, which respects their terms. Direct sources are Reddit, Hacker News, App Store reviews, GitHub issues and public pricing pages.

    How many tokens does it use?

    A quick run used about 100k tokens in our tests, on your agent's normal usage. Raw web pages are saved to disk instead of read into the conversation, and extras like the landing page are only built when you ask.

    How long does a run take?

    Quick mode took 2 to 6 minutes in our tests. Our timed deep run took 29 minutes: it scouted 7 niches, read over 1,400 pages and checked 78 quotes. You can stop and resume any run.

    Can it help a business I already run?

    Yes. "Beat my competitors" mode skips the idea hunt and goes straight to what competitors' customers complain about and how to win them over.
