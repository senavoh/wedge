Verdict

PIVOT

The pain is real: six different Skimmer reviewers describe repeated price hikes, and another app's owner-operator with 20 stops saw his bill go from $65 to $115. But "cheaper" is already for sale (Paythepoolman at $50 + $15 per tech, QuoteIQ from $29.99) and Skimmer's own $1-per-pool tier is cheap for a small route, so a price wedge alone is thin. Pivot the angle to an offline-first, fast route log with a written price lock for owner-operators, and test it through your cousin's network before building. Customer voice comes from App Store reviews only (Reddit and the review sites blocked this network), so treat it as directional.

The case against

A separate critic pass argued against this. Here is what it found.

Risk: Cheaper alternatives already exist: Paythepoolman ($50 + $15/tech), QuoteIQ (from $29.99), and at least nine new pool-route apps in the App Store with almost no ratings.

Answer: Unresolved.

Risk: Skimmer's own Getting Started tier is $1 per pool, so a solo with a small route already pays little; the price wedge is thin.

Answer: Unresolved.

Risk: Switching cost: owners have years of customer and service data in Skimmer (one reviewer says so).

Answer: Only one reviewer raised it (e22); free CSV import and a done-for-you migration address it directly.

Risk: Nobody pays for this.

Answer: They pay today and complain about the amount, not about paying (e3, e8, e9).

Risk: Someone tried and died.

Answer: One search found no shutdowns, only consolidation (Fluidra bought Pooltrackr). The category is being bought, not abandoned.

Risk: Skimmer can cut small-route prices whenever it wants.

Answer: Unresolved.

08

Pricing and cost

Pricing

Flat $29 to $39/mo per company up to ~75 pools, price-locked; test the number in interviews

Must at least match QuoteIQ and Paythepoolman on price, so price is a floor, not the wedge; the lock and the offline reliability are the pitch.

Competitors: $1 to $2 per pool (Skimmer, PoolDial), $15 to $65 per tech, $29.99+ per user

09

7-day test

One experiment, one pass bar. Run it before writing code.

Ten 20-minute calls with owner-operators via your cousin, showing a clickable offline route-log prototype and a price-lock offer.

Pass bar (7 days): 5 of 10 agree to pre-pay the first month or join a paid pilot

  • Day 1: ask your cousin for 10 intros to solo or 1-3 truck owners
  • Day 2-3: build a clickable prototype of the offline route log and invoice
  • Day 4-6: run the calls; ask what they pay now, last price notice, and what broke last week
  • Day 7: count pre-pay commitments against the pass bar

Kill it if

  • Fewer than 3 of 10 owners will pre-pay
  • Most owners say Skimmer's $1 tier or Paythepoolman is fine for them
  • Nobody names a reliability or offline problem unprompted
