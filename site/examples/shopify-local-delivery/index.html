01

Verdict

PIVOT

The pain is real: merchants say date-picker apps get skipped at checkout, break themes, answer support slowly, and in Zapiet's case cost too much. But the gap is not open. Pickeasy (4.9, 1,327 reviews), Buunto, Bird and Scrollengine already sell slots and daily capacity for $0 to $20 a month, and the critic found the route-sheet add-on pivot already served too. Don't build a general scheduling app. Spend one week testing the add-on by hand with the two florists, and drop it if they won't pay.

03

The gap

The opening competitors leave, backed by what their customers say.

Small food and flower shops on Shopify that take dated local orders can't count on a picker that forces a date or slot without breaking their theme, because the cart-widget apps they use can be skipped at checkout or clash with themes. Well-rated incumbents and Shopify's validation Functions mean this is fixable by anyone, not an open gap.

The case against

A separate critic pass argued against this. Here is what it found.

Risk: Crowded category (about 70 apps) where apps rated 4.8-5.0 with free tiers already do slots, capacity, and routes with drivers.

Answer: Unresolved.

Risk: The route and production-sheet pivot is already served: EasyRoutes is free up to 50 orders a month and includes pick and packing lists, Mipler sells a delivery-date production report, and Zapiet exports CSV by delivery date.

Answer: Unresolved.

Risk: Any incumbent can copy it in a week (a Liquid template or a grouped CSV).

Answer: Unresolved.

Risk: The Built for Shopify badge needs 50 net paid installs and 5 reviews; the incumbents already have it.

Answer: Unresolved.

Risk: An add-on depends on each picker app's attribute names, and an upstream change breaks it.

Answer: Unresolved.

Risk: Others have died here: Shopify's own Local Delivery app (around Jan 2023), and reportedly a delivery-date app removed in 2025.

Answer: Unresolved.

Risk: Shopify ships a free native date picker.

Answer: Shopify's local delivery docs cover zones and pricing only, and Shopify closed its own driver app. It has moved away from this space.

09

7-day test

One experiment, one pass bar. Run it before writing code.

Make each florist's real delivery-day sheet by hand from their Shopify orders for one week, then ask them to pre-pay for an app that does it.

Pass bar (7 days): Both florists say they tried EasyRoutes Free and a CSV export and still want it, and at least one pre-pays $9 or more

  • Day 1: ask both florists what they print or write down each delivery morning
  • Day 2: have them try EasyRoutes Free and their picker's CSV export
  • Days 3-6: hand-make their daily sheet from real orders
  • Day 7: ask for a pre-payment

Kill it if

  • Neither florist pre-pays
  • EasyRoutes Free or a CSV export covers their need
  • Fewer than 3 more merchants show interest from Community answers within 2 weeks
