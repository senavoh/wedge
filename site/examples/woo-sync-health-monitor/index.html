01

Verdict

PIVOT

The pain holds up. Different store owners, across four sync plugins and three sites, describe jobs that stall for days while dashboards say there are no errors (e1, e7, e10). But the monitor as pitched doesn't clear GO. Nobody visibly pays for monitoring on its own: the closest WP.org twin, CatalogCanary, has fewer than 10 installs, and a Shopify monitor has 0 reviews. Feed vendors already email on failed runs. Google pushes approval changes for free. Reading Meta-side status needs App Review. The pivot left to test is an agency multi-site watchdog that reads local jobs only, pre-sold before any build.
