Honest FAQ

Don't Ticket Spot and Ticket Tailor already do this?

They offer hosted checkout on WordPress for organizers. We're focused on agencies running many client sites from one dashboard. If you manage one site, they may suit you better.

My client is a registered nonprofit. Why not Zeffy?

If your client is happy on Zeffy's free hosted pages, they probably should use it.

What if you shut down?

Ticket money goes straight into your client's Stripe account, never ours, and you can export all data as CSV at any time.

Do you do calendars, seating or recurring events?

Not at launch. We only sell and deliver tickets. Keep your calendar plugin.
