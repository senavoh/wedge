Offers that win switchers

  • Done-for-you migration from Amelia or Bookly with a before/after count
  • Bookings never stop if your license lapses
  • Renewal price is the price you paid, printed on the pricing page
  • Named developer, first reply within 1 business day (Mon-Fri)
  • Automatic table backup and one-click rollback before every update
  • 30-day refund, no argument

7-day test

One experiment, one pass bar. Run it before writing code.

Offer a $149 done-for-you migration or rescue to people with a recent public Amelia/Bookly problem, and collect waitlist signups from two honest guides.

Pass bar (7 days): 3 people agree to pay (or deposit) for a $149 migration, or 25 waitlist signups with 5 yes replies to $79/yr

  • Day 1: buy steadyslots.com, deploy landing/index.html, build the reviewer list
  • Day 2: send message A to 15 reviewers via their own sites
  • Day 3: publish the rollback guide; answer 5 unresolved WP.org threads with no pitch
  • Day 4: send message B to 10 freelancers; quote $149 on jobs.wordpress.net posts
  • Day 5: publish the migration guide and the vs pages (with untrue promises removed)
  • Day 6: join r/WPAmelia and the Amelia Discord within their rules; do a timed migration dry run
  • Day 7: count signups, replies and paid asks; re-score willingness_to_pay

Kill it if

  • Under 10 waitlist signups after 7 days and 25 direct messages
  • 0 people willing to pay for a migration or rescue at any price you name
  • Most replies say they already moved to LatePoint, SSA or Fresha
  • Contacted reviewers say the vendor has since fixed their problem
  • Migration dry runs take more than 4 hours each, which breaks 15 h/week at $149
