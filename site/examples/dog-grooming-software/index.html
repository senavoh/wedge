01

Verdict

PIVOT

The pain is real but narrow. About 15 different groomers in App Store reviews complain about slow, buggy apps on the road, wrong addresses, metered texts and payment fees, and charges after cancelling. But a general 'cheaper MoeGo' is already taken: MoeGo is well rated, and ZendPaw ($39) and BooknBuddy (from $29.99) already pitch solo groomers and MoeGo switchers. Only build if the speed-on-the-road angle passes a 10-groomer interview test first. Evidence comes from App Store reviews only; Reddit, Capterra, G2 and Trustpilot were blocked.

06

The case against

A separate critic pass argued against this. Here is what it found.

Risk: Crowded at the low end: ZendPaw ($39) and BooknBuddy ($29.99) already sell cheap solo plans and target MoeGo switchers, so 'cheaper MoeGo' is not a gap.

Answer: Unresolved.

Risk: MoeGo is well liked: 4.59 across 297 ratings, with most recent reviews 5-star.

Answer: Partly. The complaints that do exist cluster on speed and addresses while mobile (e9, e11, e12), a narrow angle MoeGo's marketing doesn't lead with. That is a hypothesis to test, not a proven gap.

Risk: A free generic substitute (Square Free) exists.

Answer: Not grooming-specific: no pet records or routing (e30).

Risk: Switching cost: client and pet history is hard to move, and a failed import made a groomer cancel.

Answer: Doing the import by hand for the first users turns this into an offer (e18).

Risk: iOS-only leaves out Android groomers, and their clients will need a web booking page anyway.

Answer: Unresolved.

Risk: Someone tried and died: the search found no shutdowns.

Answer: No dead competitors found; the market is alive and crowded instead.

09

7-day test

One experiment, one pass bar. Run it before writing code.

Your partner interviews 10 solo mobile groomers from their Facebook groups: current app, all-in monthly cost, top 2 annoyances. No pitch and no links.

Pass bar (7 days): 6 of 10 name speed or addresses on the road among their top 2 annoyances, AND 3 say they'd switch for $29 flat with import done for them, AND fewer than half are already happy on ZendPaw, BooknBuddy or similar

  • Day 1-2: partner posts a no-link question in 3 groups, books 10 calls
  • Day 3-5: calls, log answers in a sheet
  • Day 6: ask the 3 most interested for a $29 pre-order or a migration slot
  • Day 7: score against the pass bar

Kill it if

  • Fewer than 6 of 10 rank on-the-road speed or addresses in their top 2
  • Most are already happy with a $30-40 alternative
  • Nobody pre-orders
