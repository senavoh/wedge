Honest FAQ

Stockroom is free. Why would I pay you?

You might not need to. If your vendors are already set up as suppliers and your label printer works, install it yourself. You'd pay us for the hours: mapping vendors, bringing over history, testing labels, training staff.

Is this an app?

No. It's a one-time setup on tools you choose. Nothing locks you in.

My Stocky data might be gone. Can you still help?

Yes. A vendor's migration checklist says Shopify's read-only window ends November 29, 2026. After that, we rebuild from your Shopify product export and supplier invoices.

Will you touch my live inventory?

Only after you sign off. We test on one location first.
