01

Verdict

PIVOT

The pain is real and recent. Shopify shut Stocky on Aug 31 2026, and retailers can't print labels for what they just received on native purchase orders. But at least 8 apps already chase that exact gap, some for free, so a new public app has no opening. Pivot to a fixed-fee migration and receiving/label setup for small POS retailers, built on the new PO Read API, and only productize if 3 or more pay.
